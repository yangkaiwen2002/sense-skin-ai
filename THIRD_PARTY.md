# Artwork attribution

The compact URL catalog in `frontend/src/utils/skinImages.json` is derived from the public [ByMykel/CSGO-API catalog](https://github.com/ByMykel/CSGO-API) (`public/api/en/skins.json`, accessed 2026-10-09). The catalog project uses the MIT license. Images are loaded directly from Valve's Steam CDN; artwork and game trademarks belong to their respective owners. No artwork is bundled in this repository.

Mappings preserve the legacy demo's English market-name associations. Demo labels are not an authoritative item catalog. Missing matches (including the legacy “AWP | 超导体” entry) deliberately show an unavailable-image placeholder. Art depicts a finish, not a specific float or pattern. The design is independently implemented; no BUFF or Youpin assets or code are included.
